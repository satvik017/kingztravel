Beach & Islands
6 Days / 5 Nights
Andaman, India
Andaman Island & Beach Escape
1N Port Blair • 2N Havelock • 1N Neil • 1N Port Blair
  • Port Blair, Havelock & Neil Island
  • Radhanagar Beach & Coral Reefs
  • Cellular Jail Light & Sound Show
  • Ferry Transfers & Stays
6-Day Plan
Day 01 Arrive Port Blair – Corbyn's Cove & Cellular Jail

Arrive at Port Blair, transfer to hotel. Visit coconut palm fringed Corbyn's Cove Beach. Later, visit historic Cellular Jail ('Kala Paani') and experience the moving Light & Sound Show. Overnight at Port Blair.

Day 02 Port Blair to Havelock Ferry – Radhanagar Beach

Take luxury tourist ferry to Havelock Island (57 km). Visit world-famous Radhanagar Beach, renowned for crystal turquoise waters, white sand curve & lush greenery. Overnight at Havelock Island.

Day 03 Kalapathar Beach Trip at Havelock

Excursion to scenic Kala Patthar Beach, famous for black volcanic rocks against turquoise sea, hammocks, and picture-perfect backdrop. Overnight at Havelock.

Day 04 Havelock to Neil Island – Laxmanpur & Natural Bridge

Morning ferry to Neil Island. Visit Laxmanpur Beach and witness the natural geological marvel of the Living Coral Natural Bridge (subject to low tide). Overnight at Neil Island.

Day 05 Neil Island to Port Blair Return Ferry

Visit Bharatpur Beach, ideal for swimming and optional coral reef sports (glass bottom boat, jet ski, snorkeling). Evening ferry back to Port Blair. Overnight at Port Blair.

Day 06 Port Blair Hotel to Airport Drop

After breakfast, transfer to Veer Savarkar Airport, Port Blair for return flight back home with wonderful island memories.

Adventure
6 Days / 5 Nights
Leh Ladakh, India
Leh Ladakh Adventure Escape
3N Leh • 1N Nubra • 1N Pangong
  • Leh, Nubra Valley & Pangong Lake
  • Khardung La Pass (17,582 ft)
  • Double-Humped Camel Safari
  • Monasteries & Local Culture
6-Day Plan
Day 01 Arrive in Leh (3,524 m) – Acclimatization

Arrive at Leh Airport, meet representative and transfer to hotel. Rest full day for altitude acclimatization. Evening short walk at Shanti Stupa for sunset. Overnight in Leh.

Day 02 Leh Sights – Hall of Fame, Magnetic Hill & Sangam

Visit Hall of Fame war museum, gravity-defying Magnetic Hill, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, and Indus-Zanskar river confluence (Sangam). Overnight in Leh.

Day 03 Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La (17,582 ft)

Drive over one of the world's highest passes. Visit Diskit Monastery (106-ft Buddha statue) and enjoy double-humped camel safari on Hunder dunes. Overnight in Nubra.

Day 04 Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake via Shyok Route

Scenic drive to world-famous Pangong Tso (14,270 ft). Witness changing hues of blue across India and Tibet borders. Overnight in deluxe lakeside camp.

Day 05 Pangong Sunrise to Leh via Chang La Pass

Witness sunrise over Pangong Lake. Drive back to Leh across Chang La Pass (17,590 ft). En route visit Thiksey Monastery and Shey Palace. Overnight in Leh.

Day 06 Leh Airport Drop

Transfer to Leh Airport for return flight with lifelong Himalayan memories.

6-Day Plan
Day 01 Arrive Bali (DPS) – Transfer to Seminyak Resort

Arrive at Ngurah Rai Airport, Denpasar. Flower garland welcome, transfer to resort. Evening stroll along Seminyak Beach with sunset drinks. Overnight in Bali.

Day 02 Tanjung Benoa Watersports & Uluwatu Kecak Dance

Watersports at Tanjung Benoa (Banana boat included). Later, visit Uluwatu Cliff Temple 70m above Indian Ocean waves. Watch sunset Kecak & Fire Dance. Overnight in Bali.

Speedboat excursion to Nusa Penida: iconic Kelingking T-Rex cliff, Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong infinity pool, and Crystal Bay with buffet lunch. Overnight in Bali.

Drive to Kintamani overlooking Mount Batur crater lake. Visit Tegalalang Rice Terraces, coffee plantations, and the famous Bali Jungle Swing. Check into Ubud resort.

Explore Ubud Sacred Monkey Forest and Tegenungan Waterfall. In the evening, visit offshore Tanah Lot Temple for world-famous sunset ocean silhouettes. Overnight in Ubud.

Day 06 Ubud Leisure to Denpasar Airport Departure

Breakfast at resort. Free time for Ubud Art Market handicrafts until private transfer to Denpasar Airport (DPS) for departure.

Family Holiday
6 Days / 5 Nights
Kerala, India
Kerala Backwaters Family Holiday
01N Cochin • 02N Munnar • 01N Thekkady • 01N Alleppey
  • Munnar, Thekkady & Alleppey
  • Traditional Houseboat Stay
  • Tea Gardens & Waterfalls
  • Periyar Wildlife Lake Safari
6-Day Plan
Day 01 Arrival at Cochin & Sightseeing

Arrive at Cochin port-city, meet representative and transfer to hotel. Visit Folklore Museum, Jew Town, Jewish Synagogue, Dutch Palace, St. Francis Church, Chinese Fishing Nets, and Marine Drive. Overnight in Cochin.

Day 02 Cochin to Munnar Drive (4 Hrs)

Drive to the hill station of Munnar ('Nature Lover’s Paradise') through mist-clad tea gardens. Visit Cheeyappara and Valara Waterfalls en route. Overnight in Munnar resort.

Day 03 Munnar Local Sightseeing & Tea Gardens

Visit Echo Point, Kundala Dam, Mattupetty Lake & Dam, Tea Museum & Factory, Rose Garden, Pothamedu Viewpoint, and Eravikulam National Park (Nilgiri Tahr habitat). Overnight in Munnar.

Day 04 Munnar to Thekkady & Periyar Safari (4 Hrs)

Drive to Thekkady. Embark on a boat safari on Periyar Lake inside Periyar National Park wildlife sanctuary (elephants, sambar). Visit spice plantations and Kathakali show. Overnight in Thekkady.

Day 05 Thekkady to Alleppey Houseboat (5 Hrs)

Drive to Alleppey ('Venice of the East'). Check into traditional luxury Kerala houseboat. Cruise through lush backwaters, Vembanad Lake, Kuttanad paddy fields, and enjoy sunset. Overnight on houseboat.

Day 06 Alleppey to Cochin Departure (2 Hrs)

Breakfast on houseboat. Check out and drive to Cochin International Airport for onward departure with cherished memories of God's Own Country.
