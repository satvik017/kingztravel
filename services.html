Accommodation

From local sightseeing and popular tourist attractions to city sightseeing and planned sightseeing tours, your experiences can be organized around your travel schedule and interests.

Food & Dining

Good food adds another experience to every journey. Kings Travels can help coordinate restaurant and dining services at your destination according to your itinerary and selected travel plan. GOOD TO KNOW

Good to Know Before You Go

1. What travel services does Kings Travels provide in India?

Kings Travels provides travel planning support for transfers, accommodation, sightseeing and dining arrangements. Our travel services in India can be planned around your destination, travel dates, itinerary and individual requirements.

2. Does Kings Travels provide airport pickup and drop?

Yes. Kings Travels can help arrange airport pickup and drop, railway station transfers, hotel transfers and other transportation required according to your travel itinerary.

3. Can I arrange an airport-to-hotel transfer?

Yes. An airport-to-hotel transfer can be arranged according to your arrival details, destination and itinerary. Hotel-to-airport transfers can also be coordinated for your return journey.

4. Can Kings Travels help with room booking?

Yes. Kings Travels can assist with room booking and accommodation arrangements. Depending on your destination and travel requirements, options can include hotels, resorts, camps and villas.

5. Is sightseeing included in your travel services?

Sightseeing can be planned according to your selected itinerary. Depending on the destination, this may include local sightseeing, city sightseeing, popular tourist attractions and other destination experiences.

6. Can I customize my sightseeing itinerary?

Yes. Your sightseeing plan can be organized around your interests, available time and travel itinerary. You can share the attractions and experiences you would like to explore while planning your journey.

7. Do you arrange food and dining during a trip?

Kings Travels can help coordinate restaurant and dining services during your journey. Exact dining or meal arrangements depend on your destination, itinerary and selected travel plan.

8. Can transfers, accommodation and sightseeing be arranged together?

Yes. Transfers, accommodation, sightseeing and dining arrangements can be coordinated around the same itinerary, helping you keep the essential parts of your journey organized.
