HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is included in an international tour package?
An international tour package can include flights, hotel accommodation, airport transfers, sightseeing and selected activities depending on your chosen itinerary. Visa assistance, meals, travel insurance and additional experiences may also be included or added depending on the destination and package.
2. Can I customize my international tour package?
Yes. Your package can be customized according to your destination, travel dates, trip duration, preferred flights, hotel category, sightseeing interests and budget. You can start with an existing package or ask Kings Travels to create an itinerary around your own international travel plans.
3. Which international destinations are popular for travellers from India?
Dubai, Thailand, Bali, Singapore, Maldives, Vietnam, Sri Lanka and destinations across Europe are popular choices for international trips from India. The right destination depends on your budget, travel month, trip duration and the type of experience you want.
4. How early should I book an international tour package?
It is generally better to start planning international travel early, particularly when your destination requires a visa or you intend to travel during a peak holiday period. The ideal booking window depends on your destination, visa requirements, flight availability and travel dates.
5. Do international tour packages include flights and visa assistance?
Flights can be included or arranged depending on your chosen package. Kings Travels can also provide guidance with tourist visa requirements and documentation where applicable. Always check the exact package inclusions before confirming your booking.
6. Can you plan international holidays for families, couples and groups?
Yes. Kings Travels can plan international holiday packages for couples, honeymooners, families, friends and groups. The itinerary, stays, transfers and experiences can be adjusted according to the people travelling, destination, trip duration and budget.
