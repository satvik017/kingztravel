Quick answers to help you plan your trip with ease.

For hill stations like Kashmir, Himachal and Ladakh, summer (March – June) offers great weather, while winter (December – February) is ideal for snowfall. Tropical places like Kerala, Dubai, Thailand and Rajasthan are best explored between October and March.

Start with your travel style (relaxation, adventure, cultural sightseeing or family bonding), your available vacation dates, and your budget. Our travel specialists can recommend destinations that match your preferences perfectly.

For domestic trips across India (Kashmir, Kerala, Goa, Himachal), 5 to 7 days is usually the sweet spot. For nearby international spots like Dubai, Thailand or Singapore, 4 to 6 days works wonderfully, while European holidays typically require 10 to 14 days.
