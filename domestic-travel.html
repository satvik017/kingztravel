HELP & INFORMATION

FAQs About Domestic Tour Packages

View All FAQs
1. What is included in a domestic tour package?
A domestic tour package can include hotel accommodation, transfers, sightseeing and selected activities based on your itinerary. Flights, meals and additional experiences can also be added depending on your destination and travel requirements.
2. Can I customize my domestic tour package?
Yes. Your package can be customized according to your destination, travel dates, number of days, hotel preferences, sightseeing interests, transportation requirements and budget.

You can start with an existing package or ask Kings Travels to create an itinerary around your own travel plans.
3. Which are the best destinations for domestic holidays in India?
The right destination depends on the experience you want and the time of year you plan to travel.

Kashmir and Himachal Pradesh are popular for mountain holidays, Kerala for backwaters and nature, Andaman for beaches and islands, Sikkim for Himalayan scenery and Leh Ladakh for road trips and adventure.
4. How early should I book a domestic tour package?
Booking early generally gives you more choice of hotels and transportation, particularly during school holidays, festivals, long weekends and peak travel periods.

For popular destinations during peak season, planning further in advance can also help you secure better availability.
5. Do you offer domestic tour packages for families and groups?
Yes. Kings Travels can plan domestic tour packages for families as well as domestic group tour packages for friends, extended families, corporate teams and other groups.

Packages can be customized with suitable stays, transportation, sightseeing and experiences according to the size and requirements of your group.
6. Can flights, hotels and transfers be included in domestic travel packages?
Yes. Domestic travel packages can include hotel stays, airport or railway station transfers, local transportation, sightseeing and selected activities.

Domestic flight assistance and additional experiences can also be added depending on your itinerary and requirements.
